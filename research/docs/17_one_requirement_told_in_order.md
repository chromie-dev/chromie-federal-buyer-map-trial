# One requirement, told in the order the evidence arrived

This document follows a single Navy requirement through the public record in the order the record was
made, and states after each step what the evidence to that point supports. Every date, amount, name and
identifier below is taken from a saved document with a hash and a retrieval time. Nothing is asserted that the record does not state.

The requirement is the Navy's small form factor weapons data link radios for the Multifunctional
Information Distribution System (MIDS). The program office that owns it is PMW 101, under PEO C4I and the
Naval Information Warfare Systems Command (NAVWAR). The contracting office that signs for it is NAVWAR's
headquarters contracting office, N00039.

## 1. The older record: a program that buys in cycles (2014 to 2015)

SAM.gov holds the office's own history. On 2014-01-21 the contracting office N00039 posted a
presolicitation for MIDS Joint Tactical Radio System (JTRS) terminals under solicitation N00039-14-R-0021,
and on 2014-12-17 a presolicitation for MIDS Low Volume Terminals (LVT) under N00039-15-R-0222, whose text
names PMW 101 as the requiring office. Award notices followed on 2015-06-02 and 2015-06-16 for the first and
on 2015-08-24 for the second.

What is inferred: MIDS is one program with two terminal lines, JTRS and LVT. The office that owns the
requirement is PMW 101 and the office that signs is N00039, and the two must not be confused. Each line is
bought on a cycle of presolicitation, solicitation and award that runs from eight months to a year and a
half.

## 2. The first weapons data link contracts and the first technical statement (2019 to 2020)

The Federal Procurement Data System records the base awards N00039 signs. On 2019-12-30 it signed
N0003920F0050 with Rockwell Collins, Inc. for TACNET 1.1 weapons data link (WDL) terminal radios. This is
the first appearance of the weapons data link in the record as an item the office procures, and it appears
with the vendor's name attached.

On 2020-05-06 the Navy pre-released SBIR topic N202-135, Model Based Systems Engineering for Tactical Data
Link Systems, sponsored by PMW 101, and on 2020-08-25 topic N203-150, Frequency Hopping Optimization for
Tactical Data Links, sponsored by NAVWAR. A topic is the office stating a technical problem it wants solved
and is prepared to fund a small company to work on.

What is inferred: as of 2020-05-06, three independent kinds of evidence spoke about the same requirement
under the same office, the notice history, the contract record and the office's own technical statements.
Three independent kinds is the threshold at which the monitor treats a requirement as live. The
presolicitation with which this account ends was posted 2,289 days later. The figures in this account count
statements this old toward the threshold.

## 3. The market is consulted and the incumbents are renewed (2022 to 2023)

On 2022-05-27 N00039 posted a request for information, NAVWAR MIDS JTRS: Embedded National Tactical Radio.
On 2022-09-23 it signed N0003922F4034 for MIDS-LVT(1) BU2 production units, ending 2025-03-31, and on
2022-10-26 posted a combined synopsis and solicitation for MIDS On Ship. On 2022-11-07 it signed
N0003923F4003 with Rockwell Collins for TACNET 1.1 WDL terminal radios, ending 2025-04-19.

On 2023-05-05 it signed N0003923F4026 with L3 Technologies, Inc. for SF1 investigation requests and
maintenance, ending 2026-05-04. SF1 is the designation the office would later use for the first small form
factor radio family; it appears here in a contract description three years before the production notice.

On 2023-06-20 NAVWAR released its long range acquisition estimate, the annual spreadsheet of what each
office expects to buy. Three rows belong to PMW 101: MIDS-LVT IDIQ, new contracts, under requirement number
N00039-23-RFPREQ-PMA/PMW-101-0017; MIDS FMS program support services; and MIDS engineering and logistics
recompete, 21F3011 follow-on. On 2023-08-25 N00039 posted a sources sought for MIDS; on 2023-12-05 it signed
N0003924F4020 with Rockwell Collins for TACNET 1.5 WDL terminal radios; and on 2023-12-14 it posted
presolicitation N0003924R4000.

What is inferred: the office was moving the weapons data link from the 1.1 to the 1.5 radio, the LVT line
was headed for a new multiple award contract, and every weapons data link contract to date had gone to one
vendor. The forecast confirmed what the notices and contracts implied and named the owning office directly.

## 4. The requirement acquires its name (2024)

On 2024-01-17 N00039 posted a request for information for MIDS SF2, and on 2024-02-09 solicitation
N0003924R4000. On 2024-06-20 the next long range acquisition estimate carried a row placed at PEO C4I, MIDS
WDL SF, beside PMW 101's rows for the MIDS JTRS base IDIQ, the MIDS-LVT Lot 25 procurement buy, the
recompete and the FMS support. On 2024-07-30 N00039 posted presolicitation N0003924R4100, MIDS Weapons Data
Link Small Weapons Attritable (SWARMM) Family 2 (SF2) Radio Development and Production; on 2024-07-31 a
request for information for SF2 radio software; and on 2024-08-30 the solicitation. On 2024-12-04 the Navy
pre-released SBIR topic N251-017, Small Type-1 Encryption for Aircraft, Littoral, and Terrestrial
Higher-than-Secret Applications, sponsored by PMW 101.

What is inferred: the small form factor radios had become a named line of their own in the forecast; the
office had defined a family of them, SF1 and SF2 with SF3 and SF4 to follow; the second family was in
development and production; and the office was funding small companies on the encryption problem such a
radio carries. The terms that identify every later statement about this requirement, MIDS, WDL, SWARMM and
the SF designations, were all public by the end of 2024.

## 5. The vehicle, the incumbents' end dates and the third family (2025)

On 2025-04-02 the Navy pre-released topic N252-D11, Advanced Interference Mitigation Techniques, sponsored
by PMW 101 and open directly at Phase II. On 2025-04-09 N00039 posted a presolicitation for MIDS Low Volume
Terminals under N00039-24-R-4019, with the solicitation on 2025-05-02, and signed N0003925F4018 with
Rockwell Collins for TACNET 1.5 WDL terminal radios, ending 2027-09-30. On 2025-05-14 it posted a request
for information for MIDS WDL SF4 radios.

On 2025-06-19 the long range acquisition estimate placed MIDS WDL SF2 Production at PEO C4I under
N00039-24-RFPREQ-PEO-C4I-0012 and, at PMW 101, Delivery Order 0002 under SF1 contract
N0003923D4000_N0003925F4027, under N00039-25-RFPREQ-PMA/PMW-101-0046. The forecast thereby named the
contract vehicle for the first family, and the office's rows in that release carried a secondary point of
contact, Chalon D James.

On 2025-07-17 N00039 signed N0003925F4027 with L3 Technologies for SF1 radio development, ending
2027-07-16. On 2025-08-13 and 2025-09-25 it signed three further TACNET 1.5 orders with Rockwell Collins,
N0003925F4016, N0003925F4017 and N0003925F4051, all ending 2027-09-30. On 2025-08-28 it posted a sources
sought for the MIDS SWARMM Family 3 radio. On 2025-09-29 it signed N0003925F4056 with Rockwell Collins for
MIDS WDL SF2 radio production, ending 2027-08-31.

What is inferred: by the autumn of 2025 the first family belonged to L3 Technologies under N0003923D4000,
the second to Rockwell Collins, a third was being scoped and a fourth had been asked about, and the current
radio orders all ended within weeks of one another in September 2027. A prospective vendor for the next
family could identify the owning office, the contract vehicle, the incumbents, the dates on which the
incumbents' work ran out, and the technical problems the office was paying to have studied.

## 6. The funding line, the notices and the people (2026)

On 2026-02-05 N00039 posted a sources sought, MIDS Weapons Data Link Small Form Factor Weapons Attritable
Radio Multi-Mode (SWARMM) Family 1 (SF1) Radio Production, under a solicitation number that carries
PMW 101's own requirement identifier, N0003925RFPREQPMAPMW1010223. On 2026-03-26 a presolicitation for
security modules supporting MIDS LVT production was placed at PEO C4I. On 2026-04-20 two award notices
announced the MIDS LVT multiple award contract for production and sustainment.

On 2026-04-30 the Department of the Navy's Other Procurement justification book for fiscal year 2027
entered the record. Line 2614, Advanced Tactical Data Link Systems, carries $58.739 million for fiscal year 2026 and
$52.758 million for fiscal year 2027, and its justification names MIDS in the on-ship configuration; line
3620, Coast Guard Equipment, also names MIDS. The funding evidence is adjacent rather than exact: the line
funds the Link 16 family to which the radios belong rather than the radios by name.

On 2026-05-13 and 2026-06-18 N00039 signed N0003926F4007 and N0003926F4006 for MIDS WDL systems
engineering and integration for the small form factor radios, ending in May and June 2028, and on
2026-06-15 N0003926F4008 for SF2 radio production lot 2, ending 2027-12-31. On 2026-07-29 it posted a
request for information for SF4 radio cryptographic device solutions.

On 2026-08-12 N00039 posted presolicitation N00039PRESOL_SF3, MIDS Weapons Data Link Small Form Factor
Weapons Attritable Radio Multi-Mode (SWARMM) Family 3 (SF3) Radio, whose text names PMW 101. The primary
point of contact is Damon R Griffin. On 2026-08-27 a special notice on a MIDS JTRS IDIQ ceiling increase
named Lindsay Nickel as its point of contact.

What is inferred: the third family's presolicitation, the event a vendor would have prepared for, arrived
with two years of public warning in the forecast, the contract record and the office's technical
statements, and with the names of the contracting staff who would answer questions about it.

## 7. The monitor's reading

Read against the record above, the requirement had five kinds of evidence in place: the forecast, the
contract record, the notices, the office's technical topics and a funding line. Its score was 99.73 of 100,
made up of 40 of 40 for breadth of evidence, 19.73 of 20 for recency, 20 of 20 for persistence and 20 of 20
for proximity. In the most recent quarter the record gained nine statements against seven in the quarter
before. No new kind of evidence had appeared in ninety days.

The monitor's action for this requirement was a meeting with PMW 101. It attached one statement from each
kind of evidence and the three people the record ties to the office: Lindsay Nickel and Damon R Griffin
from the 2026 notices and Chalon D James from the 2025 forecast release. Five further actions watched the
contracts whose end dates fall within the horizon, the TACNET 1.5 radio orders ending 2027-09-30 and the
SF1 development order ending 2027-07-16. The vendors in the office's record for this requirement were Data
Link Solutions L.L.C. with 66 base awards, Viasat, Inc. with 55, L3 Technologies, Inc. with 22, EuroMIDS
with 17 and Rockwell Collins, Inc. with 10.
